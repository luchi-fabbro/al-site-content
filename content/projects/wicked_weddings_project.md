In 2017, through Partini, I launched Wicked Weddings LA — horror-themed wedding ceremonies performed in the gothic garden at SugarMynt Gallery in South Pasadena, officiated by none other than Michael Myers himself.

The hook was both conceptual and genuinely geographical: SugarMynt Gallery at 810 Meridian Avenue sits in the shadow of the actual house used in John Carpenter's *Halloween* (1978). Couples were literally eloping to Haddonfield.

## The concept

Civil ceremonies for the not so faint of heart. Thirty minutes of terrifying bliss. Legal ceremonies performed by Sinister Ministers, with Michael Myers or the Wicked Widow officiating — couples could choose their villain, or let fate decide.

Each package included the ceremony, wedding cupcakes, sparkling mini toasts, certificate signing, and a ceremony photograph. Vow renewals and commitment ceremonies were also available for couples who wanted the experience without the legal paperwork.

The setting was a fully dressed private gothic garden: black twisted branches, crimson floral arrangements, candlelight, purple uplighting, and Michael Myers standing between the urns where your photographer would be expecting a traditional officiant.

## The venue and the trail

SugarMynt Gallery's private garden, which Partini branded as Haddonfield Place for the Wicked Weddings events, was transformed into a fully immersive Halloween set. The original Michael Myers house — as filmed by John Carpenter for the 1978 classic — stands nearby on the same street in South Pasadena. For horror fans, getting married there was not just a ceremony. It was a pilgrimage.

Couples arrived at Haddonfield Place and were greeted by a Sinister Minister who reviewed their LA County marriage license. They walked down the aisle to their choice of music and exchanged vows — standard or their own — before being pronounced by Michael Myers or the Wicked Widow. The ceremony was followed by cake, a sparkling toast, and the certificate signing.

Two witnesses were required to sign the license alongside the ordained minister. A professional photographer captured the ceremony, with additional time available to book in advance.
